How to download a Snapchat Spotlight video

Spotlight is Snapchat’s public feed of short videos. Because those videos are public, you can save one with just its link.

Steps

  1. Find the video. Open the Spotlight video you want in Snapchat.
  2. Copy the link. Tap Share, then Copy Link. The link usually starts with snapchat.com.
  3. Paste and download. Paste the link into Snapsaver, tap Get video, check the preview and tap Download video.

Open Snapsaver

What you get

You get the video file Snapchat provides for that Spotlight video, saved as an MP4 you can play on your phone.

If the video doesn’t appear

  • The video may have been removed or made private. Try a different one.
  • Copy the link again and paste the whole thing.
  • Wait a moment and try again if Snapchat is slow to respond.

Respect the creator

Save Spotlight videos for personal use. If you want to share one, share the original link, or ask the creator first.
