Snapsaver

Save Snapchat videos and stories from a link.

Paste the link, tap Get video, then download. Works with public Spotlight videos and public stories.

How it works

  1. 1
    Copy the linkIn Snapchat, open the video or story, tap Share, then Copy Link.
  2. 2
    Paste it aboveTap Paste, then Get video.
  3. 3
    DownloadCheck the preview and tap Download video.

We don’t keep anything.

Your video is never saved on our servers. To get it again, paste the link again.

Questions

Why can’t I save a private story?

Private snaps and friends-only stories can’t be reached from a link. Snapsaver only works with public content.

Do you store my videos?

No. Nothing you download is saved on our servers.

Can I save the same video again later?

Yes. Paste the same Snapchat link again and tap Get video.

The link doesn’t work. What now?

Make sure the video or story is public, then copy the link again from the Share menu. Links must come from snapchat.com.

Snapsaver is not affiliated with Snap Inc. Videos belong to their creators. Save them for personal use and don’t repost without permission.
