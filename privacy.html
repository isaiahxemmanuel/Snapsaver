Privacy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Your links and videos

When you paste a link, our server uses it only to find the public video and send it to you. We don’t save the video, and our code doesn’t keep a record of the links you paste. Each download passes straight from Snapchat’s servers to your device.

Visit counts (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to count visits and understand how the site is used. It records things like which pages are viewed, your approximate country (worked out from your IP address), your device and browser type, and how long a visit lasts. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to do this.

To learn how Google uses data from sites like this one, see Google’s explanation. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Technical logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to run and secure the service.

Other services

This site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to fetch them.

Your Snapchat account

We never ask for your Snapchat username or password. You only paste a link. If a site claiming to be Snapsaver asks for your login, it isn’t us.

Not affiliated with Snap Inc.

Snapsaver isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by Snap Inc. Videos belong to their creators. Save them for personal use and don’t repost without permission.
