How to save a Snapchat story

You can save a public Snapchat story with nothing more than its link. Here’s how, step by step.

Steps

  1. Copy the link. In Snapchat, open the public story, tap Share, then Copy Link. Menus change between Snapchat versions, so look for the share arrow.
  2. Paste it into Snapsaver. Open Snapsaver, paste the link into the box and tap Get video.
  3. Download. A story with several snaps shows each one separately. Tap Download on the ones you want.

Open Snapsaver

Which stories can be saved

Only public stories, such as those on a public profile. Private snaps and friends-only stories can’t be saved from a link, and Snapsaver never asks for your Snapchat login.

If it doesn’t work

  • Check that the story is public and still available. Many stories disappear after 24 hours.
  • Copy the link again from the Share menu, then paste the whole link.
  • Links copied from private chats usually won’t work.

Be kind to creators

Stories belong to the people who made them. Save them for personal use, and ask before you repost anything.
